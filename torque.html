Engineering Formula

Moment of force relationship

T = F × r × sin(θ)

T = Torque

F = Force

r = Moment arm / distance

θ = Angle between F and r

How it is calculated

  1. Convert force into Newtons.
  2. Convert distance into metres.
  3. Convert the angle from degrees to radians.
  4. Calculate sin(θ).
  5. Multiply F × r × sin(θ).

Engineering Information

Understanding torque in mechanical systems.

What is Torque?

Torque is the turning effect produced by a force about an axis or pivot point.

It determines how strongly a force tends to rotate an object.

SI Unit

The SI unit of torque is the Newton-metre (N·m).

Torque depends on both force and its distance from the axis of rotation.

Why Angle Matters

Maximum torque occurs when the force acts at 90° to the moment arm because sin(90°) = 1.

At 0°, the turning effect is zero.
