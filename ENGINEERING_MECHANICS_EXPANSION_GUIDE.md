## Design and integration

- Existing `style.css` and `calculator.css` are preserved.
- `engineering-mechanics.css` is additive and contains only subject-specific visual styles.
- Every new calculator includes step-by-step working, engineering assumptions, a visual problem diagram, and `saved-calculations.js` integration.
- Homepage search/filter cards and Engineering Mechanics filter were added.
- Sitemap and README were updated.
