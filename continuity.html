Continuity Equation

A₁V₁ = A₂V₂

For steady incompressible flow, the volume flow rate remains constant.

Q = AV

Where:
A = Cross-sectional area
V = Fluid velocity
Q = Volumetric flow rate

Important Principle

If the flow area decreases, velocity increases for steady incompressible flow.

If the flow area increases, velocity decreases.

Understanding the Continuity Equation

The continuity equation is based on conservation of mass. For steady incompressible flow, it becomes:

A₁V₁ = A₂V₂

  • Smaller area: Higher fluid velocity.
  • Larger area: Lower fluid velocity.
  • Volumetric flow rate: Q = AV
  • SI unit of Q: m³/s

This calculator assumes steady incompressible flow.
