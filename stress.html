σ = Stress

Important:
1 N/mm² = 1 MPa

About Stress

Stress is the internal resisting force developed within a material when an external force acts on it.

For a uniformly loaded component, normal stress is calculated by dividing the applied force by the cross-sectional area.

  • Tensile stress occurs when a component is pulled.
  • Compressive stress occurs when a component is pushed.
  • Increasing the applied force increases stress.
  • Increasing the cross-sectional area reduces stress for the same applied force.
