Understanding Hydraulic Power

Hydraulic power represents the rate at which energy is transferred by a pressurized flowing fluid.

The basic relationship is: P = ΔP × Q

  • Higher pressure: More hydraulic power for the same flow rate.
  • Higher flow rate: More hydraulic power for the same pressure.
  • SI unit: W
  • Common engineering unit: kW

This calculator gives ideal hydraulic power. Actual pump or motor input power will be higher because of efficiency losses.
