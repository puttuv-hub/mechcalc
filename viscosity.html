Understanding Dynamic Viscosity

Dynamic viscosity describes the internal resistance of a fluid when adjacent layers move relative to each other.

For a Newtonian fluid, shear stress is directly proportional to the velocity gradient.

  • Low viscosity: Fluid flows more easily.
  • High viscosity: Fluid offers greater resistance to flow.
  • SI unit: Pa·s
  • Common unit: mPa·s or cP
  • 1 mPa·s: 1 cP
