Understanding Kinematic Viscosity

Kinematic viscosity is the ratio of dynamic viscosity to fluid density.

It represents the diffusion of momentum through a fluid and is particularly useful in fluid-flow calculations.

  • Dynamic viscosity: Resistance of a fluid to shearing.
  • Kinematic viscosity: Dynamic viscosity divided by density.
  • SI unit: m²/s
  • Common unit: cSt
  • 1 cSt: 1 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s
