Engineering Formula

Mechanical power relationship

P = W / t

P = Power

W = Work / Energy

t = Time

How it is calculated

  1. Convert work or energy into Joules.
  2. Convert time into seconds.
  3. Divide work by time.
  4. The result is power in Watts.

Engineering Information

Understanding power in mechanical engineering.

What is Power?

Power is the rate at which work is done or energy is transferred.

For mechanical systems: P = W / t

SI Unit

The SI unit of power is the Watt (W).

1 W = 1 J/s

Common Applications

Power calculations are used for motors, engines, pumps, turbines, compressors, generators and machine drives.
