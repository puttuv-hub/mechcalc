Engineering Formula

Newton's Second Law of Motion

F = m × a

F = Force

m = Mass

a = Acceleration

How it is calculated

  1. Convert the mass into kilograms.
  2. Convert the acceleration into m/s².
  3. Multiply mass by acceleration.
  4. The result is force in Newtons.

Engineering Information

Understanding force in mechanical engineering.

What is Force?

Force is a physical interaction that can change the motion of an object. In classical mechanics, force is calculated using Newton's Second Law: F = m × a.

SI Unit

The SI unit of force is the Newton (N).

1 N = 1 kg·m/s²

Common Applications

Force calculations are used in machine design, structural analysis, vehicle engineering, mechanisms, lifting equipment and dynamics.
