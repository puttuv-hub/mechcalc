Specific Gravity is the ratio of the density of a substance to the density of a reference fluid.

For liquids and solids, water is commonly used as the reference fluid.

Understanding Specific Gravity

Specific gravity (SG) is a dimensionless quantity because it is a ratio of two densities having the same units.

It helps engineers compare the density of a material with a reference substance, commonly water for liquids and solids.

  • SG < 1: Substance is less dense than the reference fluid.
  • SG = 1: Substance has the same density as the reference fluid.
  • SG > 1: Substance is denser than the reference fluid.
