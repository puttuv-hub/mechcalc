Engineering Formula

Pressure relationship

P = F / A

P = Pressure

F = Force

A = Area

How it is calculated

  1. Convert force into Newtons.
  2. Convert area into m².
  3. Divide force by area.
  4. The result is pressure in Pascals.

Engineering Information

Understanding pressure in engineering.

What is Pressure?

Pressure is the amount of force acting perpendicular to a surface per unit area.

It increases when the same force acts over a smaller area.

SI Unit

The SI unit of pressure is the Pascal (Pa).

1 Pa = 1 N/m²

Common Applications

Pressure calculations are used in hydraulics, pneumatics, pressure vessels, pumps, compressors, engines and fluid systems.
