Understanding Head Loss

Head loss represents the loss of mechanical energy experienced by a flowing fluid because of friction between the fluid and the pipe wall.

The Darcy-Weisbach equation is: hf = f(L/D)(V²/2g)

  • Longer pipe: increases head loss.
  • Smaller diameter: increases head loss significantly.
  • Higher velocity: increases head loss approximately with V².
  • Higher friction factor: increases head loss.

This calculator calculates major pipe friction loss. Minor losses from valves, bends, fittings, entrances and exits are not included.
