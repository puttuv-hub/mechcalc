Mechanical Engineering Formulas

Fundamental mechanical engineering equations used by students and engineers.

Stress

σ = F / A

Strain

ε = ΔL / L₀

Young's Modulus

E = σ / ε

Axial Deformation

ΔL = PL / AE

Thermal Stress

σ = EαΔT

Poisson's Ratio

ν = −εₗ / εₓ

Elastic Constants

G = E/[2(1+ν)]

K = E/[3(1−2ν)]

Bending Stress

σ = M / Z

Torsion

T/J = τ/R = Gθ/L

Principal Stress

σ₁,₂ = C ± R

Thin Cylinder

σh = pd/2t

σl = pd/4t

Euler Buckling

Pcr = π²EI/(KL)²

Strain Energy

U = P²L/(2AE)

Beam Deflection

δ ∝ Load × L³ or L⁴ / EI

Force

F = m × a

Pressure

P = F / A

Power

P = W / t

Torque

T = F × r × sin(θ)

Work

W = F × d × cos(θ)

Kinetic Energy

KE = ½ × m × v²

Weight

W = m × g

Mechanism Mobility

F = 3(n−1) − 2J₁ − J₂

Slider-Crank Velocity

v = rω[sinθ + sin2θ/(2n)]

Gear Ratio

N₂/N₁ = Z₁/Z₂

Planetary Gears

(ωr−ωc)/(ωs−ωc) = −Zs/Zr

Belt Friction

T₁/T₂ = e^(μθ)

Flywheel Energy

ΔE = Iω²Cs

Watt Governor

ω² = g/h

Gyroscopic Couple

C = IωΩ

Natural Frequency

ωn = √(k/m)

Damping Ratio

ζ = c/(2√km)

Forced Vibration

M = 1/√[(1−r²)²+(2ζr)²]

Ideal Gas

PV = mRT

First Law

Q − W = ΔU

SFEE

q − w = Δh + Δke + Δpe

Isentropic Gas

T₂/T₁ = (P₂/P₁)^((γ−1)/γ)

Entropy Change

Δs = cp ln(T₂/T₁) − R ln(P₂/P₁)

Otto Efficiency

η = 1 − 1/r^(γ−1)

Carnot Efficiency

η = 1 − T_L/T_H

Rankine Efficiency

η = W_net/Q_in

Pump Euler Head

H = (U₂Vw₂ − U₁Vw₁)/g

Pump Hydraulic Power

P = ρgQH

Pump Specific Speed

Nₛ = N√Q/H^(3/4)

NPSH Available

NPSHₐ = (Pₛ−Pv)/(ρg) + z − hf

Turbine Euler Work

w = U₁Vw₁ − U₂Vw₂

Turbine Specific Speed

Nₛ = N√P/H^(5/4)

Turbine Unit Speed

Nu = N/√H

Draft-Tube Recovery

h ≈ (V₁²−V₂²)/(2g)

Thoma Coefficient

σ = (Ha−Hv−Hs)/H

Jet Force

F = ṁ(Vin−Vout)

Density

ρ = m / V

Specific Weight

γ = ρ × g

Specific Gravity

SG = ρsubstance / ρreference

Dynamic Viscosity

μ = τ / (du/dy)

Kinematic Viscosity

ν = μ / ρ

Reynolds Number

Re = ρVD / μ

Bernoulli Equation

P/ρg + V²/2g + z = constant

Continuity Equation

A₁V₁ = A₂V₂

Hydraulic Power

P = ΔP × Q

Pump Power

Ph = ρgQH

Pipe Flow Rate

Q = AV

Q = πD²V / 4

Head Loss

hf = f(L/D)(V²/2g)

Mechanical Efficiency

η = (Pout / Pin) × 100

Engine Displacement

Vₛ = (π/4)D²L × n

Compression Ratio

r = (Vₛ + Vc) / Vc

Brake Power

BP = 2πNT / 60

Mechanical Efficiency — Engine

ηm = BP / IP × 100

Brake Thermal Efficiency

ηbth = BP / (ṁf CV)

BSFC

BSFC = ṁf / BP

Volumetric Efficiency

ηv = ṁa / (ρ Vd × intake cycles/s)

Mean Piston Speed

Cm = 2LN / 60
