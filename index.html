Mechanical Engineering Formulas

Fundamental mechanical engineering equations used by students and engineers.

Stress

σ = F / A

Strain

ε = ΔL / L₀

Young's Modulus

E = σ / ε

Force

F = m × a

Pressure

P = F / A

Power

P = W / t

Torque

T = F × r × sin(θ)

Work

W = F × d × cos(θ)

Kinetic Energy

KE = ½ × m × v²

Weight

W = m × g

Density

ρ = m / V

Specific Weight

γ = ρ × g

Specific Gravity

SG = ρsubstance / ρreference

Dynamic Viscosity

μ = τ / (du/dy)

Kinematic Viscosity

ν = μ / ρ

Reynolds Number

Re = ρVD / μ

Bernoulli Equation

P/ρg + V²/2g + z = constant

Continuity Equation

A₁V₁ = A₂V₂

Hydraulic Power

P = ΔP × Q

Pump Power

Ph = ρgQH

Pipe Flow Rate

Q = AV

Q = πD²V / 4

Head Loss

hf = f(L/D)(V²/2g)

Mechanical Efficiency

η = (Pout / Pin) × 100
