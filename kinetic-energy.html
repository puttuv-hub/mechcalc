Engineering Formula

KE = ½ × m × v²

Where:

  • KE = Kinetic Energy
  • m = Mass
  • v = Velocity

Engineering Information

Kinetic energy is the energy possessed by an object because of its motion.

The SI unit of kinetic energy is the joule (J).

Kinetic energy depends on the square of velocity. Therefore, increasing velocity has a significant effect on energy.

KE ∝ v²

Common Units

  • 1 kJ = 1,000 J
  • 1 MJ = 1,000,000 J
  • 1 J = 1 N·m
