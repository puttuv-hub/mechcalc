Calculation Steps

Engineering Formula

W = F × d × cos(θ)

Where:

  • W = Work
  • F = Force
  • d = Displacement
  • θ = Angle between force and displacement

Engineering Information

Mechanical work is performed when a force causes an object to move through a displacement.

The SI unit of work is the joule (J).

When force and displacement are in the same direction, θ = 0° and:

W = F × d

When the force is perpendicular to displacement, θ = 90° and the mechanical work is zero.

Common Units

  • 1 kJ = 1,000 J
  • 1 MJ = 1,000,000 J
  • 1 J = 1 N·m
