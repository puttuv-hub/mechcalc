Engineering Formula

ρ = m / V

Where:

  • ρ = Density
  • m = Mass
  • V = Volume

Engineering Information

Density is the mass contained in a unit volume of a substance.

The SI unit of density is:

kg/m³

A material with a higher density contains more mass in the same volume compared with a lower-density material.

Approximate Material Densities

  • Steel ≈ 7850 kg/m³
  • Aluminium ≈ 2700 kg/m³
  • Copper ≈ 8960 kg/m³
  • Water ≈ 1000 kg/m³
