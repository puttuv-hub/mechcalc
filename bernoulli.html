Bernoulli Equation

P₁/ρg + V₁²/2g + z₁ = P₂/ρg + V₂²/2g + z₂

This form expresses conservation of mechanical energy per unit weight for ideal steady incompressible flow.

Assumptions

  • Steady flow
  • Incompressible fluid
  • Negligible friction losses
  • No pump or turbine energy added or removed

For real systems, pump head, turbine head and head losses may need to be included.

Understanding Bernoulli's Equation

Bernoulli's equation relates pressure energy, kinetic energy and potential energy along a streamline.

  • Pressure head: P / ρg
  • Velocity head: V² / 2g
  • Elevation head: z

The three terms have units of metres of fluid head.
