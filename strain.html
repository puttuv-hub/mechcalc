Engineering Formula

ε = ΔL / L₀

Strain = Change in Length ÷ Original Length

Where:

ε = Strain

ΔL = Change in Length

L₀ = Original Length

Important:
Strain is dimensionless because length units cancel.

Strain can also be expressed as a percentage:
Strain (%) = Strain × 100

About Strain

Strain represents the deformation of a material relative to its original length.

Unlike stress, strain has no physical unit because it is the ratio of two lengths.

  • Tensile strain occurs when a material elongates.
  • Compressive strain occurs when a material shortens.
  • Strain is a dimensionless quantity.
  • Strain is commonly expressed as a decimal, percentage or microstrain (με).
Example: If a 1000 mm rod increases in length by 2 mm:

ε = 2 / 1000 = 0.002
Strain = 0.002
Strain = 0.2%
