Understanding Pump Power

A pump must supply energy to a fluid to overcome elevation, pressure and flow resistance.

The ideal hydraulic power is: Ph = ρgQH

Because a real pump has efficiency losses, the actual power required at the pump input is: Pin = Ph / η

  • Higher flow rate: increases required power.
  • Higher pump head: increases required power.
  • Higher fluid density: increases required power.
  • Higher efficiency: reduces required input power for the same hydraulic output.

Pump efficiency must be entered as a percentage. For example, 80% efficiency is entered as 80.
