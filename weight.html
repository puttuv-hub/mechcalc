Calculation Steps

Engineering Formula

W = m × g

Where:

  • W = Weight in newtons (N)
  • m = Mass in kilograms (kg)
  • g = Gravitational acceleration (m/s²)

Engineering Information

Weight is the gravitational force acting on an object.

Near the Earth's surface, standard gravitational acceleration is approximately:

g = 9.81 m/s²

Unlike mass, weight depends on the local gravitational acceleration.

For example, an object has the same mass on Earth and the Moon, but its weight is different.

Common Units

  • Weight → N
  • 1 kN = 1,000 N
  • 1 MN = 1,000,000 N
  • Mass is measured in kg
