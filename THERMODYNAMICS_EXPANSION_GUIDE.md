# MECHCALC Thermodynamics Expansion

This offline build adds a new **Thermodynamics** category with 23 B.Tech-focused problem-solving calculators.

## Topics covered
- Ideal-gas properties and specific heats
- First Law for closed and steady-flow systems
- Constant-volume, constant-pressure, isothermal, polytropic and isentropic processes
- Entropy change
- Carnot, Otto, Diesel, Dual and Brayton cycles
- Simple Rankine cycle
- Nozzle/diffuser, turbine, compressor, throttling and mixing chamber
- Steam quality / dryness fraction

All new pages preserve the existing MECHCALC CSS architecture and load `saved-calculations.js` for Firestore save/history support.
