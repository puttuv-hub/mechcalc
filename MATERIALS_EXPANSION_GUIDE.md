# Engineering Materials / Metallurgy Expansion

This checkpoint adds 29 student-focused materials and metallurgy calculators. Each page includes formulas, assumptions, input validation, step-by-step working, visual engineering context and Save Calculation support.

Topics: crystal structures and diffraction; mechanical tests and hardness; strengthening; fracture, fatigue and creep; diffusion and carburizing; phase fractions and carbon equivalent; composites; thermal expansion; corrosion and impact energy.
