About Young's Modulus

Young's modulus is a measure of the stiffness of a material within its elastic range.

It represents the relationship between normal stress and normal strain in the linear elastic region of a material.

  • A higher Young's modulus generally indicates a stiffer material.
  • A lower Young's modulus indicates that the material deforms more for the same stress.
  • Young's modulus is commonly expressed in GPa.
  • The relationship applies in the linear elastic region.
Typical approximate values:

Steel ≈ 200 GPa
Aluminium ≈ 69 GPa
Copper ≈ 110 GPa
