Calculation Steps

Engineering Formula

γ = ρ × g

Where:

  • γ = Specific weight
  • ρ = Density
  • g = Gravitational acceleration

Engineering Information

Specific weight is the weight of a substance per unit volume.

Its SI unit is:

N/m³

In engineering applications, specific weight is commonly expressed as kN/m³.

For water near standard conditions:

γ ≈ 9.81 kN/m³

Important Relationship

  • Density → kg/m³
  • Specific weight → N/m³
  • Specific weight = density × gravity
