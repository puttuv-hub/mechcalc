Formula

Q = A × V

For a circular pipe:

A = πD² / 4

Therefore:

Q = πD²V / 4

Where:

Q = Volumetric flow rate

A = Pipe cross-sectional area

D = Internal pipe diameter

V = Average fluid velocity

Understanding Pipe Flow Rate

Volumetric flow rate represents the volume of fluid passing through a pipe cross-section per unit time.

For a circular pipe: Q = A × V

Since the pipe area is: A = πD² / 4, the flow rate becomes: Q = πD²V / 4.

  • Larger diameter: increases flow rate at the same velocity.
  • Higher velocity: increases flow rate for the same diameter.
  • Internal diameter: use the actual inside diameter of the pipe.
  • Velocity: use the average fluid velocity across the pipe.

This calculator determines geometric volumetric flow rate. It does not calculate pressure loss, pump head, friction loss or compressible-flow effects.
