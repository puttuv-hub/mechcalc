Engineering Information

Why Unit Conversion Matters

Engineering equations require compatible units. Converting values to a consistent unit system helps prevent calculation errors.

  • Always check units before calculating.
  • Use SI units when possible.
  • Keep track of area and volume conversions carefully.
  • Remember that squared and cubed units require special conversion factors.

Common SI Units

  • Length → metre (m)
  • Area → square metre (m²)
  • Volume → cubic metre (m³)
  • Mass → kilogram (kg)
  • Force → newton (N)
  • Pressure → pascal (Pa)
  • Power → watt (W)
  • Energy → joule (J)
